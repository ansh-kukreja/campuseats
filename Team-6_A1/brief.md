# About CampusEats

CampusEats is a campus food-ordering platform which allows students to order food online from multiple food outlets within a university campus, such as cafeterias, food courts all through a single app. Instead of physically standing in line at each outlet, a student can see the menu, places an order, pays digitally and picks up the food when it's ready.

## Who uses it ?
- **Students** : The primary users are Students that are enrolled in that University/College, they will place orders and collect their food.
- **Outlet Staff** : Food outlets that receive orders, prepare food and manage orders.
- **Campus Administration** : Manages the outlets and monitors overall usage.
